# Sticky Space Paws

A hand-tracked VR game for Meta Quest: you are a space badger with sticky paws, raiding bins at night. Turn the junk and the food to fit the holes in the walls, eat what is food, throw away what is not.

Play it in the Quest browser: open this site's page and press PLAY. Hand tracking only; clear some space around you.

Made with three.js (MIT). Music and pictures by the author, made with AI tools. See LICENCES.txt and privacy.html.
