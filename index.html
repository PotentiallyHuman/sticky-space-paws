You are a space badger. The bins are full, your paws are sticky, and the neighbours are very light sleepers.

Everything else is inside: the menu opens in the headset, worked with your fingers or a controller.

Hands only. This cannot be played with controllers, and you need both hands and most of your fingers.
Clear some space round you, take breaks, and stop if you feel unwell.

This browser cannot open a headset. On a Meta Quest, open this page in the headset's own browser and press PLAY.

Settings

These are also in the headset's own menu.

Re-centre at any time: hold your right index finger and thumb together, or pick up a controller and hold the Meta button.

How to play

1Turn what you find so it fits the hole in the wall coming at you. The wall pushes whatever does not fit.
2Pushed through the glass? A window lights up. Three lights and the neighbour comes out.
3When the lid opens: throw junk away (not at the house), bring food to your face and eat it.
  • Your paws are sticky with honey: a touch pulls, a shake lets go. In the open lid the honey boils off junk.
  • A tinted hole wants the matching colour facing it: that colour eats the barrier away.
  • Later bins add gravity, side walls, two at once, chopsticks, and a joker in every bin that plays by its own rule.
  • A clean round after a light puts that light out again. A new bin starts with all lights off.
