Sticky Space Paws: privacy

This game collects nothing and sends nothing.

  • Your hands are tracked by your headset so that the paws can follow them. That happens on the headset. The positions are used to draw and play the game and are thrown away at once; they are never stored and never leave the device.
  • The game saves two numbers on your own device so they are there next time: your best score and how many bins you have reached. You can remove them by clearing the browser's site data for this game.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no contact with any server once the game's files have loaded.
